# Awesome-Agricultural-Carbon-Management

## Top Agricultural Carbon Management Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Soil Carbon MRV, Farm Carbon Credits, Regenerative Agriculture Measurement, Carbon Marketplaces & Ag Climate Solutions*  

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Agricultural Carbon Management**. These systems help measure, report, and verify (MRV) soil and farm carbon outcomes, enroll growers in carbon programs, and connect agricultural practices to carbon markets and climate incentives.



**Examples** include Regrow Ag, Pachama, Nori, Agreena, Indigo Ag, Clearloop, Perennial, Agoro Carbon, Downforce Technologies, and CarbonSpace (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Commercial platforms dominate scaled soil-carbon programs, satellite MRV, and carbon credit issuance. Open-source activity is growing in farm-scale GHG calculators, EO-based proxy workflows, and research MRV tools—led by projects such as **Open Farm Carbon Tracker**. This section lists every significant relevant project found.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[Regrow Ag](https://www.regrow.ag/)**  

  Platform for regenerative agriculture outcomes, including soil carbon and sustainability measurement, reporting, and program support for food and ag companies.



- **[Indigo Ag, Agreena, Nori](https://www.indigoag.com/)**  

  Carbon and regenerative agriculture programs connecting growers with carbon markets through practice change, MRV, and credit generation.



- **[Pachama, Perennial, CarbonSpace, Downforce Technologies](https://pachama.com/)**  

  Technology platforms using remote sensing, modeling, and data science for forest and/or soil carbon measurement, verification, and project integrity.



- **[Agoro Carbon, Clearloop](https://agorocarbon.com/)**  

  Agricultural carbon project developers and platforms focused on soil carbon sequestration programs and credit supply.



- **[Other commercial agricultural carbon & climate platforms](https://www.regrow.ag/)**  

  Solutions supporting farm GHG accounting, regenerative practice tracking, and carbon market participation.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[Open Farm Carbon Tracker](https://github.com/euraf/OpenFarmCarbonTracker)**  

  Open-source tool for diagnostics of farm-scale greenhouse gas emissions. Aims to leverage open datasets for baseline scenarios and support transparent farm carbon accounting (MIT).



- **[EO-based soil carbon proxy / MRV workflows](https://github.com/search?q=soil+carbon+MRV+OR+soil+carbon+satellite)**  

  Research and portfolio projects demonstrating Earth Observation workflows (e.g., Sentinel-2 indices + climate data) for cropland vegetation and soil proxies relevant to MRV-style analysis.



- **[Blue carbon & ecosystem MRV prototypes](https://github.com/search?q=blue+carbon+MRV+OR+carbon+registry+open+source)**  

  Open experiments in blockchain-backed or multi-source MRV for coastal and other ecosystem carbon projects.



- **[Farm carbon calculator & GHG inventory open tools](https://github.com/search?q=farm+carbon+OR+agricultural+GHG+calculator+open+source)**  

  Community and research calculators implementing IPCC-style or regional emission factors for farm-level GHG estimation.



- **[Agricultural carbon credit / registry PoCs](https://github.com/search?q=agricultural+carbon+credit+OR+soil+carbon+platform)**  

  Prototype platforms exploring farmer onboarding, practice logging, satellite verification, and credit issuance workflows.



- **[Open soil & crop data pipelines](https://github.com/search?q=soil+organic+carbon+OR+Sentinel+agriculture+open+source)**  

  Tools for processing satellite and climate data that underpin many carbon monitoring approaches.



- **[COMET-style & public model interfaces](https://github.com/search?q=COMET+Farm+OR+soil+carbon+model)**  

  Open or publicly documented modeling approaches related to widely used agricultural carbon quantification methods (availability varies by jurisdiction and license).



- **[Regenerative practice tracking open apps](https://github.com/search?q=regenerative+agriculture+OR+cover+crop+tracking)**  

  Simple open applications for logging regenerative practices that can feed into broader carbon accounting workflows.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **Farm-scale GHG diagnostics**: Open Farm Carbon Tracker as a transparent starting point for farm emissions and carbon-related accounting.

- **EO research workflows**: Reproducible satellite + climate proxy analyses for educational and research MRV.

- **Composable stacks**: Practice logs + open emission factors + satellite indices + simple reporting for pilot programs.

- **Traceability linkage**: Combine with open food/ag supply-chain tools when carbon claims need farm-to-buyer provenance.

- Full commercial platforms remain far ahead in scaled grower networks, credit registry integration, and operational MRV.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**:  

**Open Farm Carbon Tracker** and related open GHG/EO tools provide the best current open foundations for farm-level carbon and emissions diagnostics.  

Research MRV workflows and calculators support pilots and education.  

Commercial platforms (Regrow, Indigo, Agreena, Nori, Pachama, Perennial, etc.) deliver grower enrollment at scale, satellite and model-based MRV, carbon credit pathways, and buyer programs.  

Most large agricultural carbon initiatives rely on commercial platforms; open tools are valuable for research, transparency, smallholder pilots, and custom regional methodologies. Fully open end-to-end carbon credit systems are not yet mature for major registries.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Agricultural carbon claims and credits are subject to evolving scientific, methodological, and registry requirements. Inaccurate MRV can undermine climate integrity and create legal or reputational risk.

- Open-source tools offer transparency but generally lack the operational scale, registry integration, and third-party verification processes of commercial programs. Do not issue or sell carbon credits based solely on unvalidated open tools without appropriate methodology approval and verification.
